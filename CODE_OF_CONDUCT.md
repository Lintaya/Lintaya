# Lintaya community code of conduct

English | [Español](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.es.md)

## Our commitment

We want Lintaya to be a welcoming, technically rigorous community where people
can participate regardless of experience, identity, background or location.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence.
- Make room for newcomers and explain project-specific context.
- Protect private information and immediately report accidental disclosure.
- Accept moderation decisions and corrections professionally.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained
disruption, impersonation and publishing another person's private information
without permission are not tolerated. Security concerns must not be used to
intimidate or publicly shame contributors.

## Scope

This policy applies in repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, events
and other spaces where someone represents the Lintaya project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Do not open a public issue
that exposes the reporter or affected person. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation or permanently ban
an account according to severity and history.

Maintainers will avoid conflicts of interest and handle reports with discretion.
Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith is prohibited.
